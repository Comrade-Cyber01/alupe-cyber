Registering a business name (Sole Proprietorship or Partnership) in Kenya is the fastest way to formalize your hustle. In 2026<\/strong>, the process is fully handled through the Business Registration Service (BRS) V2<\/strong> on the eCitizen portal.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

It costs exactly KES 950<\/strong> and takes about 3 to 5 working days<\/strong>. Here is the punchy, step-by-step breakdown.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

\n\n\n\n

Step 1: The Name Search (Merged)<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n

In the new system, name reservation and registration are merged.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  1. Log into your eCitizen account<\/a>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  2. Select Business Registration Service (BRS)<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  3. Click Make Application<\/strong> and select Registration of a Business Name<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  4. Enter 3 to 5 preferred names<\/strong> in order of priority. The system will check these against the database in real-time.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n

    Step 2: Business Details & Nature<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n

    Once you’ve submitted your names, you\u2019ll need to provide the “meat” of the business:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

      \n
    • Nature of Business:<\/strong> Be specific (e.g., “General Supplies” or “Software Consultancy”).<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Address:<\/strong> You must provide a physical location (Building name, Street, Town). If you work from home, use your residential address.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Contact Info:<\/strong> An active email and phone number are mandatory.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

      Step 3: Ownership Information<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n
        \n
      • Sole Proprietorship:<\/strong> Enter your ID\/Passport number and KRA PIN.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Partnership:<\/strong> You will need the details (ID, PIN, and Photos) for all partners.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Note:<\/strong> The system will pull your data automatically from the Integrated Population Registration System (IPRS).<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

        Step 4: Sign & Upload the BN2 Form<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n

        This is where most people get stuck.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

          \n
        1. The portal will generate a Business Name Registration Form (BN2)<\/strong> based on your data.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        2. Download it<\/strong>, print it, and sign it manually<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        3. Scan the signed form (as a PDF) and upload it back to the portal.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n

          Step 5: The Payment<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n
            \n
          • The total fee is KES 950<\/strong> (KES 150 for the search and KES 800 for the registration).<\/li>\n\n\n\n
          • Pay via M-Pesa (Paybill 222222)<\/strong> or Card.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
          • Once paid, the status will change to “Submitted.”<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

            Step 6: Download Your Certificate<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n

            Check your portal after 3\u20135 business days. Once approved, you will see a Certificate of Registration (Form BN\/3)<\/strong>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

              \n
            • Download and laminate it.<\/strong> You\u2019ll need this for your bank account and County Single Business Permit.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
              \n\n\n\n

              Pro-Tips for 2026<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n
                \n
              • Avoid Generic Names:<\/strong> Names like “Quality Stores” will be rejected instantly. Be unique\u2014add a prefix or a distinct word.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
              • KRA Integration:<\/strong> For a Business Name (Sole Prop), you do not<\/strong> get a new KRA PIN. You use your personal one for tax purposes.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
              • The “Verified” Tag:<\/strong> Ensure your eCitizen profile is “Verified” (with a green tick) before starting, or the system won’t let you proceed with BRS services.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
              • Mobile Filing:<\/strong> You can track your application status via the M-Service App<\/strong>, but the initial document upload is much easier on a desktop.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n","protected":false},"excerpt":{"rendered":"
