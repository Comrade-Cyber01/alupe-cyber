Filing your KRA Nil returns is essentially telling the taxman, “I didn’t make enough to be taxed, but I’m still a law-abiding citizen.” It takes less than five minutes and saves you from a 2,000 KES<\/strong> penalty.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Since we are in 2026<\/strong>, you are currently filing for the 2025<\/strong> calendar year.<\/sup> Here is the punchy, no-nonsense guide to getting it done.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

\n\n\n\n

Before You Start<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n
    \n
  • KRA PIN:<\/strong> Your 11-digit identifier.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Password:<\/strong> If you forgot it, use the “Forgot Password” link to get a reset one in your email.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Basic Math:<\/strong> You’ll need to solve a simple arithmetic problem (the “Security Stamp”) to log in.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
    \n\n\n\n

    The 6-Step Quick File<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n
      \n
    1. Log In:<\/strong> Head to the iTax Portal<\/a>. Enter your PIN, click Continue<\/strong>, enter your password, solve the math challenge, and hit Login<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    2. Navigate to Returns:<\/strong> On the top red menu bar, hover over Returns<\/strong> and select the last option: File Nil Return<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    3. Select Obligation:<\/strong>\n
        \n
      • The “Type” and “Taxpayer PIN” will be pre-filled.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • For the “Tax Obligation” dropdown, select Income Tax – Resident Individual<\/strong>. Click Next<\/strong>.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Confirm the Period:<\/strong>\n
          \n
        • For the 2026 filing season, the Return Period From<\/strong> should be 01\/01\/2025<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • The Return Period To<\/strong> should be 31\/12\/2025<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Note: If you have a “Wife PIN,” you can usually leave that field blank unless you are filing jointly.<\/em><\/li>\n<\/ul>\n<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Submit:<\/strong> Click the Submit<\/strong> button. A pop-up will ask if you are sure; click OK<\/strong>.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Download the Receipt:<\/strong> Once successful, you\u2019ll see a “Download Returns Receipt” link. Click it.<\/strong> This is your only proof of filing\u2014save it in your Google Drive or email it to yourself.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n
          \n\n\n\n

          Pro-Tips for 2026<\/strong><\/h3>\n\n\n\n
            \n
          • The “Update Profile” Glitch:<\/strong> If iTax forces you to “Update Professional Details” before filing, just select “Others” or your current field, save, and then go back to the Returns menu.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
          • Deadline:<\/strong> The portal gets incredibly slow in late June. Do it now to avoid the June 30th “system is busy” headache.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
